# Search intent analysis

The SERP is the only reliable statement of intent. Google has already tested what satisfies searchers; your job is to read the result and match it. A perfect page with the wrong intent will not rank.

## Intent types
| Intent | Signals | Winning page |
|---|---|---|
| Informational | what/how/why, definitions, guides | Article, guide, glossary, video |
| Commercial investigation | best, vs, review, alternatives, top | Comparison, listicle, review hub |
| Transactional | buy, pricing, trial, quote | Product, category, pricing, landing page |
| Navigational | brand or product name | Homepage, login, brand page |
| Local | map pack present, city or "near me" | Location page, Business Profile |

Many queries are mixed. Identify the dominant intent and the secondary one.

## Workflow
1. Query in a clean, logged-out browser for the target country and language. Check mobile if it may differ.
2. Record the top 10 organic results: page type, format, depth, publication date, whose site (brand, publisher, marketplace, forum).
3. Record SERP features: AI Overview, featured snippet, People Also Ask, video, images, shopping, maps, news, forum threads, sitelinks.
4. Find the pattern. Eight of ten are listicles: the intent is comparison. All are product pages: transactional. Forum threads prominent: searchers want lived experience, so first-person evidence helps.
5. Extract shared content elements across the top results (sections, tables, calculators, templates, pricing, FAQs) and the freshness expectation (current-year titles imply recency matters).
6. Find the gap or angle: what is missing, outdated, vague or generic across those results that your page can supply with first-hand evidence.
7. Decide: page type, format, primary angle, must-have sections, funnel stage, and the conversion that fits (soft CTA for informational, direct for transactional).
8. If your site already ranks, compare your page against the pattern. A mismatch (you have a blog post, the SERP wants a tool) means build the right asset, not tweak the copy.

## Edge cases
- **Split SERP:** both a guide and a product page rank. Pick the one your site can credibly serve, or build a hybrid with the guide above the fold and a product section below. Consider two pages if the keyword set justifies it.
- **Volatile intent:** news-driven or seasonal; re-check monthly.
- **AI Overview answers the query fully:** expect click loss; target the follow-up, comparison and decision queries the overview does not close.
- **Brand-dominated SERP:** a competitor's branded query is rarely worth chasing.

## Output
For each query: dominant intent, secondary intent, evidence (3-5 bullets from the SERP), recommended page type and format, must-have elements, differentiating angle, difficulty note, and an intent-match verdict for any existing URL.

## Do not
Infer intent from the keyword text alone. Do not copy the top result; match its type and satisfy the need better.

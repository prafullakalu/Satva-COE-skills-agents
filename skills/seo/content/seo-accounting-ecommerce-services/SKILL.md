# SEO for accounting and e-commerce services

Finance content is YMYL, so trust and accuracy carry more weight than volume. Buyers search by problem, software and sector; own the intersection.

## Positioning axes (pick 2-3 to build around)
- **Software:** Xero, QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books, Sage, NetSuite.
- **Sector:** Shopify and Amazon sellers, marketplaces, subscription, dropshipping, wholesale.
- **Problem:** multi-channel reconciliation, inventory and COGS, sales tax and VAT, month-end close, payout reconciliation, cleanup of messy books, ERP and platform sync.
- **Geography:** countries and states served, tax regimes handled.
- **Service:** bookkeeping, outsourced accounting, CFO, integration and automation implementation.

## Page types to build
1. **Service pages** per service x sector (`ecommerce bookkeeping for Shopify stores`) with scope, process, deliverables, pricing model or ranges, tools, SLAs and a clear enquiry path.
2. **Software-specific pages** (`Xero for e-commerce`, `QuickBooks Shopify integration setup`): real setup detail, integration pitfalls, what to map, who it suits.
3. **Problem guides** (`how to reconcile Shopify payouts in Xero`, `Amazon fees accounting treatment`, `inventory accounting for e-commerce`): step-by-step, worked examples with anonymised or synthetic figures, screenshots, common errors. These earn links and trust.
4. **Calculators and templates:** margin, fee and payout calculators, chart of accounts for e-commerce, month-end checklists.
5. **Location pages** only for places genuinely served, each with local specifics (regulation, filings, testimonials); no templated city spam (see `seo-programmatic-pages`).
6. **Case studies:** problem, approach, measurable result, client quote with permission. Never name a client without written approval.
7. **Comparison and decision pages:** in-house vs outsourced, platform A vs B for a seller profile, cost guides.

## Workflow
1. Research: `seo-keyword-research` on the axes above; examine the SERP for each money term (directories, software marketplaces and firms all appear; know which you are fighting).
2. Cluster and plan with `seo-topic-clustering`; place service pages as the commercial core and guides as supporting content that links in.
3. Apply E-E-A-T rigorously (`seo-eeat-signals`): named accountants with credentials and registration numbers where applicable, reviewer line on tax content, jurisdiction and "not advice" notes, dated and updated content, primary sources (tax authority, vendor docs).
4. Technical and local: Organization and ProfessionalService schema, Business Profile if there is a physical office, consistent name and address, reviews on independent platforms.
5. Listings and links: software partner directories (Xero and QuickBooks advisor directories, marketplace listings), professional bodies, e-commerce platform expert directories, guest commentary (`seo-link-building-digital-pr`).
6. Lead capture: intent-matched CTAs (free review, fixed-scope audit), short forms, call booking, response-time promise.
7. Measure: qualified enquiries and booked calls by page; rankings for service and software terms; branded search; review count.

## Compliance and accuracy
- Tax and accounting rules vary by jurisdiction and change; state the tax year and verify against official sources before publishing, and re-review on a schedule.
- No guarantees of savings or returns; no fabricated testimonials; follow advertising rules for regulated professions.
- Never publish client data. Use anonymised or synthetic examples.

## Output
Positioning decision, page roadmap with priorities, 90-day backlog, trust checklist, measurement plan.

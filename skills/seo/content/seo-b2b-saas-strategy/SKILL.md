# SEO for B2B SaaS

SaaS SEO is judged on pipeline, not traffic. Start where intent is closest to purchase and expand outward.

## Inputs
Product and category, ICP and buyer roles, pricing model, top 5 competitors, integrations list, sales-call objections, current analytics and CRM attribution, domain strength, content capacity.

## Priority order (build in this sequence)
1. **Foundation pages:** home, product and feature pages (one per feature or workflow, each targeting a distinct query), pricing (indexable, with plain plan detail), security and trust, customers and case studies.
2. **Bottom funnel (highest conversion):**
   - `[Competitor] alternatives` and `[Product] vs [Competitor]` pages. Be fair and specific: honest comparison tables, who each fits, migration notes. Hostile or false claims get ignored and create legal risk.
   - `Best [category] software for [segment]`.
   - Use-case and industry pages (`[category] for [industry/role]`) with real workflows and screenshots.
   - Integration pages (`[Product] + [Tool] integration`): what syncs, setup steps, limits, who benefits; unique per integration (see `seo-programmatic-pages` for scaling).
3. **Middle funnel:** how-to guides tied to the job the product does, templates, checklists, calculators, ROI tools, buyers' guides, glossary terms around the category.
4. **Top funnel:** only where it connects to the product and the audience; thought leadership with original data earns links.
5. **Docs and help centre:** index them. They capture long-tail troubleshooting and feature queries, and AI engines cite them often.

## Workflow
1. Map the buyer's journey queries to these page types, using `seo-keyword-research` and `seo-search-intent-analysis`.
2. Prioritise by value and winnability; bottom-funnel first even with low volume.
3. Write each page with a clear angle and proof: screenshots, product facts, quotes, metrics (client-approved), a single clear CTA (demo, trial).
4. Build topical depth around the category with `seo-topic-clustering` and `seo-topical-authority-plan`.
5. Earn links via data, tools, integrations and partner pages (`seo-link-building-digital-pr`).
6. Ensure technical basics: product pages server-rendered, clean URLs, fast, schema (SoftwareApplication, Organization, FAQ where visible).
7. Optimise conversion: intent-matched CTAs, short forms, demo scheduling inline.
8. Review quarterly: refresh decaying pages (`seo-content-refresh`) and check AI visibility (`seo-ai-search-visibility`).

## Measurement
Primary: organic-sourced signups, demos requested, qualified pipeline and revenue, sourced by landing page and keyword group. Secondary: rankings for bottom-funnel sets, non-brand clicks, referring domains, assisted conversions. Track branded vs non-branded separately. Ask "how did you hear about us" in forms to capture AI and dark-social influence.

## Failure modes
- Publishing top-funnel blog content with no commercial link.
- Skipping comparison pages out of caution; buyers search for them.
- Thin integration pages with identical text.
- Gating everything and hiding it from search.
- Reporting traffic while pipeline stays flat.

## Output
Strategy document: target segments, page-type roadmap with priority and owners, first 90 days backlog, measurement plan, and risks.

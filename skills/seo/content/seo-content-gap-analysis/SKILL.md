# Content gap and competitor SEO analysis

Find where competitors win organic demand that you can credibly capture, and where your existing pages underperform against theirs.

## 1. Pick the right competitors
Search competitors are domains that repeatedly appear in the SERP for your target queries; they differ from business rivals. Choose:
- 3-5 direct competitors (same product and buyer).
- 2-3 content competitors (publishers, directories, communities) that take the informational SERPs.
Take the list from SERP inspection of 15-20 core queries, not from assumption.

## 2. Collect data
Use whatever tools the team has (a third-party SEO suite, Search Console, manual SERP checks). Per competitor: ranking keywords with position and estimated traffic, top pages by traffic, content types, publishing cadence, referring domains. Record tool and date; estimates are directional.

## 3. Find gaps (four kinds)
1. **Keyword gaps:** queries where 2 or more competitors rank in the top 20 and you do not rank or rank below 20.
2. **Topic gaps:** whole subjects a competitor covers with a cluster that you lack (compare cluster structure, not single keywords).
3. **Format gaps:** competitors win with calculators, templates, comparison pages, glossary, integrations pages, tools; you only have blog posts.
4. **Depth gaps:** you rank 8-30 and the page above you is more complete, more current or better evidenced.
Also find the reverse: your strengths, to defend and extend.

## 4. Filter and score
Remove: irrelevant intent, brand terms, topics outside your credible expertise, queries owned by dominant sites you cannot beat. Score survivors using `seo-keyword-research` (business value x winnability x volume tier). Add an effort estimate (S/M/L) and a link requirement: does ranking need backlinks you do not have (see `seo-link-building-digital-pr`)?

## 5. Study the winners
For the top 10 opportunities open the competing pages: angle, structure, evidence, freshness, internal links, backlinks to the page. State what you can do materially better, since "same but longer" is not a plan.

## 6. Recommend
Group into: quick wins (improve existing pages, depth gaps), new pages (topic and format gaps), strategic bets (clusters, tools, data studies), and do-not-pursue with reasons.

## Output
- Summary: three findings and the single biggest opportunity.
- Opportunity table: topic or keyword | competitors ranking (position) | our status | intent | value | winnability | effort | action | rationale.
- Competitor snapshot: strengths, cadence, link profile notes.
- 90-day sequencing.
- Data caveats.

## Failure modes
- Copying competitors' keyword lists wholesale; they include their own mistakes.
- Judging by traffic estimates alone; check conversion fit.
- Treating a gap as an opportunity without checking you have credible expertise (E-E-A-T, see `seo-eeat-signals`).
- Comparing against sites ten times your authority and concluding every keyword is hard.

## Do not
Copy competitor content. Do not present tool estimates as measured traffic.

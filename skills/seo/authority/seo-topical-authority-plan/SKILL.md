# Topical authority planning

Topical authority is the practitioner term for being consistently useful and complete across a subject, so that search systems and readers treat you as a trustworthy source on it. It is not an official Google metric. Treat it as a planning model, and validate results with rankings.

## Workflow
1. **Pick 1-3 core topics** where the business has real expertise and revenue connection. Narrow beats broad; authority in a small territory is achievable.
2. **Map the territory.** List every question, task, comparison, problem, tool and decision around the topic, from beginner to advanced and from awareness to purchase. Sources: Search Console, autocomplete, PAA, competitor site structures, sales and support questions, forums, glossary of the field. Use `seo-keyword-research` and `seo-topic-clustering` to convert it into clusters.
3. **Score coverage.** For each cluster mark: none / thin / adequate / best-in-class, and whether the page matches intent. Count coverage by cluster, not by keyword. Identify missing foundations (definitions, how-tos) and missing advanced content.
4. **Benchmark.** Compare with 2-3 sites that already own the topic (see `seo-content-gap-analysis`): breadth, depth, freshness, link profile, author credentials.
5. **Choose the architecture.** Pillar pages per cluster family, supporting pages, glossary, tools and templates, comparison and case-study pages for commercial depth. Keep URL structure logical (/topic/subtopic/).
6. **Sequence in waves.** Wave 1: money pages and the pillar plus the most winnable supporting pages. Wave 2: fill the rest of the core clusters. Wave 3: advanced, edge cases, original research. Publish clusters together with their internal links in place.
7. **Add authority signals.** Named expert authors, reviewed content, original data and quotes, external citations of your work (see `seo-link-building-digital-pr`, `seo-eeat-signals`).
8. **Internal linking rules.** Pillar to every cluster page, clusters back to pillar, relevant siblings, descriptive anchors, and money pages linked contextually from informational content.
9. **Maintain.** Quarterly review of coverage, decay and new questions. Update pillars when sub-pages are added.

## Measure
- Share of cluster queries where you rank top 10 and top 3.
- Visibility growth across the cluster, not on a single keyword.
- New pages' time to rank (should shrink as authority grows).
- Referring domains to topic pages; branded plus topic searches.
- Conversions from the cluster.

## Output
Territory map, coverage scorecard, architecture diagram (text tree), wave plan with owners and dates, link plan, expert and evidence requirements, KPI baseline.

## Failure modes
- Publishing dozens of shallow posts to "cover" a topic.
- Wide topics without evidence of expertise.
- Building the cluster without the pillar or interlinks.
- Never updating; authority decays.

## Do not
Claim a topical authority score from a tool as fact. Do not chase topics where the business has no credible expertise.

# Link building and digital PR

Links remain a core ranking signal, and they are earned by being worth citing. Relevance and editorial placement matter more than raw count.

## 1. Audit first
- Export referring domains from Search Console (Links report) plus any third-party index the team has. Compare against 3-5 competitors (see `seo-content-gap-analysis`) to find domains linking to them and not you.
- Classify your profile: relevance, topical spread, anchor text mix (branded and URL anchors should dominate), top linked pages, and risk signals (paid or network links, spun directories, exact-match anchor spikes).
- Do not disavow by default. Use disavow only for a manual action or an obvious paid/spam scheme you cannot remove.

## 2. Choose tactics that fit the business
Ordered by durability:
1. **Linkable assets:** original research or survey data, free tools and calculators, benchmark reports, templates, definitive guides with unique visuals, glossaries with authority.
2. **Digital PR:** newsworthy data or commentary pitched to journalists and trade press; reactive expert commentary on breaking stories in your domain.
3. **Partnerships and ecosystem links:** integration directories, partner and marketplace listings, vendor customer-story pages, association memberships, sponsorships with a real editorial or resource link.
4. **Unlinked mentions and broken links:** find brand mentions without links and ask for a link; find dead links to similar content and offer yours.
5. **Expert contributions:** guest articles and podcasts on genuinely relevant, audience-bearing sites; one link in author bio is normal.
6. **Resource page and community outreach:** only for assets that deserve a spot.
Skip: link farms, PBNs, paid links without rel="sponsored", mass directory submissions, reciprocal link schemes, link exchange at scale, comment spam.

## 3. Build the asset
Ask "who would cite this and why?" Name 20 target publications before building. Include a clear methodology, quotable stats, downloadable charts, and embed or attribution instructions.

## 4. Prospect and qualify
Per target: relevance to topic, real audience, editorial standards, evidence that they link out, contact person. Disqualify sites that sell links or accept any content. Track in a sheet: domain | contact | reason relevant | asset | status | date | outcome.

## 5. Outreach
- Personalise the first line with something specific they published.
- State the value to their readers in one or two sentences, then offer the asset. Under 120 words.
- One follow-up after 5-7 days; stop after two.
- Journalists: lead with the news hook and data; offer an expert and exclusives.
Template skeleton: reference to their piece -> what we have that adds to it -> one line of proof -> easy next step.

## 6. Measure
Count only earned, followed, relevant referring domains. Track branded search growth, rankings for target pages, referral traffic, mentions and links, cost per link, and links per campaign. Review monthly; kill tactics with no yield after 2 cycles.

## Risk rules
Follow Google's link spam policies. Large unnatural jumps, sitewide footer links and exact-match anchors are patterns to avoid. Disclose sponsorships.

## Output
Link audit summary, tactic plan with targets and effort, asset ideas ranked, outreach templates, tracker structure and KPIs.

## Do not
Promise a number of links or ranking changes. Do not buy links. Do not send unpersonalised bulk outreach.

# Marketing copy — AI Accounting Agent for QuickBooks

## Feature headline
AI-powered bookkeeping, working directly with QuickBooks.

## Short product description
Satva Ledger's AI accounting agent reads your real QuickBooks transactions, proposes the
right categorization with a stated confidence level, has a second AI independently check
its own work, and only posts back to QuickBooks after you approve — then verifies the
result by reading the record back.

## One-line pitch
Three AI agents — a Bookkeeper, a Reviewer, and a QuickBooks Operator — work your books
together, and you approve every write.

## Call to action
Let your books run smarter.

## What it does (accurate, no inflated claims)
- Automates the repetitive read/categorize/compare work across real QuickBooks data
- Explains every accounting decision in plain language, with a real confidence score
- Runs every proposed change past a second, independent AI reviewer before it's shown to you
- Never writes to QuickBooks without your explicit approval
- Verifies its own write by reading the record back, not just trusting the API response
- Keeps a full audit trail of what it did and why
- Refuses to invent a number it doesn't have — says so plainly instead

## What we do not claim
- Not "100% autonomous" — every write requires human approval
- Not "zero errors" — it flags uncertainty (e.g. "55% confidence") instead of hiding it
- Not "replaces accountants" — it's a team member, not a replacement
- Not "perfect bookkeeping" — it surfaces judgment calls for a human to make

# Standard accrual and amortisation entry patterns

## Accounts payable accrual (goods or services received, not invoiced)
Dr Expense (or asset if capitalisable), Cr Accrued liabilities. Sources: open purchase orders with confirmed receipt, contracts with services rendered but unbilled, recurring vendor arrangements (utilities, subscriptions, professional fees), submitted but unprocessed employee expense reports. Auto-reverse next period. Use the same estimation method every period, document the basis (PO amount, contract terms, run-rate) and track actual versus accrual to improve estimates.

## Fixed asset depreciation
Dr Depreciation expense (by department), Cr Accumulated depreciation. Straight-line = (cost - salvage) / life; declining balance applies a fixed rate to net book value; units of production uses actual usage over expected total. Run from the register; verify additions have life and method; check disposals and impairments; keep book and tax depreciation consistent or separately tracked.

## Prepaid amortisation
Dr Expense by type (insurance, software, rent, maintenance, events), Cr Prepaid. Keep a schedule with start and end dates and monthly amounts; expense immaterial items outright; accelerate amortisation when a contract is cancelled; add new prepaids promptly.

## Payroll accruals
- Salary for pay periods not aligned with month end: Dr Salary expense (by department), Cr Accrued payroll. Base on working days in the period versus the pay period.
- Bonus: Dr Bonus expense, Cr Accrued bonus, per plan terms, targets and payout timing.
- Benefits: Dr Benefits expense, Cr Accrued benefits.
- Employer payroll tax: Dr Payroll tax expense, Cr Accrued payroll taxes. Include employer-side taxes, health and pension contributions; track leave liability where policy or law requires.

## Revenue
- Release deferred revenue: Dr Deferred revenue, Cr Revenue.
- Earned and billed: Dr Accounts receivable, Cr Revenue.
- Received in advance: Dr Cash or receivable, Cr Deferred revenue.
Contract-level detail is kept for audit; judgments on multi-element contracts belong to the revenue-recognition skills.

## Supporting documentation for every entry
Memo (what and why), calculation support, source documents (PO, invoice, contract, payroll register), period, preparer and date, approval per the matrix, reversal indicator and date.

# VAT / GST regimes: orientation table

Satva-original orientation, written from general knowledge. **Every figure is a last-known value and must be verified with the tax authority for the period in question** before it is used in a return or advice. Rates and thresholds change by budget, notification and treaty.

## Concepts that differ and cause errors

- **Zero-rated vs exempt:** zero-rated supplies allow input recovery; exempt supplies usually do not (partial-exemption apportionment applies to mixed businesses).
- **Place of supply:** goods follow location; services follow B2B customer location (reverse charge) or supplier/consumer location for B2C, with exceptions (digital services, land, events).
- **Reverse charge:** the buyer self-accounts for output and input on the same return; keep the supplier's tax number and evidence of status.
- **Import VAT:** recoverable via the return where the importer of record holds the import document (UK postponed accounting, EU variants).
- **Timing:** tax point may be invoice, delivery or payment depending on the scheme (cash accounting changes this).
- **Invoices:** a valid tax invoice is the condition for input recovery in most regimes; check mandatory fields and e-invoicing mandates.

## Review checklist for any VAT/GST return

Output tax per sales report ties to the control account; input tax is supported by valid invoices; reverse-charge entries net to zero effect where recoverable; exempt and out-of-scope sales classified; prior-period adjustments within the time limit; payment date and e-filing receipt retained; control-account closing balance equals the amount payable per the return plus items due later.

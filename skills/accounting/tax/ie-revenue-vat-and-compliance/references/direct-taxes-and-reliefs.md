# Irish direct taxes and reliefs (2025 figures; verify at revenue.ie)

## PART 3 — CORPORATION TAX (CT)

### Rates (Ireland)
| Income type | Rate |
|-------------|------|
| **Trading income** | **12.5%** |
| Non-trading income (rental, deposit interest) | 25% |
| Capital gains (within a company) | 33% |
| **Pillar Two Top-up (15% effective)** | Applies if global turnover > €750m — most SMEs not affected |
| Knowledge Development Box (KDB) | **6.25%** on qualifying IP income |

### Close Company Surcharges
A "close company" (≤5 participators controlling) faces additional surcharges if it doesn't distribute investment / professional income:
- **Investment/rental income:** 20% surcharge on undistributed amount
- **Professional services income (close company professional services co.):** 15% surcharge

**Critical for SMEs:** if you accumulate cash from professional services in the company, plan distributions (salary, dividends, pension) before year-end to avoid surcharge.

### Filing
- **Form CT1** filed via ROS
- **Filing deadline:** 9 months after year-end (no later than 23rd of that month)
- **Preliminary tax:** 90% of final CT liability (or 100% of prior year for small companies < €200k CT) due 23 days before year-end (i.e., 8 months 7 days after period start for a 12-month period)
- **iXBRL Financial Statements:** must be filed with CT1 (or within 3 months)
- **Late filing penalties:** 5% (up to €12,695) for 2 months late; 10% (up to €63,485) for over 2 months late. AND restriction of losses, capital allowances, group relief.

### R&D Tax Credit
- **25% credit** on qualifying R&D expenditure (in addition to standard 12.5% deduction → effective 37.5%)
- Refundable over 3 years if not used to reduce CT
- Must be "systematic investigation in field of science/technology, seeking advancement"
- Documentation requirements: project records, time tracking, technical narrative
- **First-year claim:** €25k pre-paid in year 1 (changed from 30/60/10 split as of 2024)
- Software development can qualify if it's genuine R&D (not routine programming)

### Trading vs investment companies
- Trading company gets 12.5% rate AND CT entrepreneur reliefs (CGT 10% on first €1m gain at sale)
- Investment company: 25% on rental, 33% CGT on disposals
- **Mixed activities:** carefully ringfence

### Common allowable deductions
- Wholly + exclusively + necessarily for trade
- Wages and salaries (including employer's PRSI)
- Subcontractor costs (RCT applies)
- Rent on commercial premises
- Insurance (commercial, professional indemnity)
- Travel for business (not commuting)
- Subscriptions to professional bodies
- Bad debts (specific provision allowed; general provision not)
- Repairs and maintenance (not improvements — those are capital)

### NOT allowable for CT
- **Entertainment** (including client entertainment — even meals) — fully disallowable
- Capital expenditure (claim via capital allowances instead)
- Owner drawings
- Penalties and fines (parking, late filing penalties, etc.)
- Political donations
- General provisions for bad debts

### Capital Allowances
- **Plant & machinery:** 12.5% straight-line over 8 years
- **Industrial buildings:** 4% over 25 years (rare)
- **Energy-efficient equipment (ACA):** 100% in year 1 (specific approved list)
- **Cars (Category A-G CO2):** 12.5% over 8 years on capped value (€24k cap for low-emission, €0 for high-emission)
- **Hybrid/EVs (BEV ACA):** 100% first-year allowance for electric vehicles + EV charging points

### Loss relief
- Trading losses set against trading profits of same period, then carry back 1 year (same trade), then carry forward indefinitely
- Group relief: surrender losses between 75%-owned group companies (Irish or EU/EEA group)

### USC (Universal Social Charge) — 2025 rates
| Band | Rate |
|------|------|
| €0 – €12,012 | 0.5% |
| €12,013 – €27,382 | 2% |
| €27,383 – €70,044 | 3% (was 4% — reduced in Budget 2025) |
| Over €70,044 | 8% |
| Self-employed > €100,000 (surcharge) | +3% (i.e., 11% on amount over €100k) |
| **Exemption:** total income < €13,000/year | Full exemption |

Medical-card holders and over-70s with income < €60,000 get reduced 2% max.

### PRSI Classes
| Class | Who | Employee rate | Employer rate |
|-------|-----|--------------|---------------|
| **A** | Most employees | 4.1% (from Oct 2024) | 11.15% (over €441/wk) / 8.9% (under) |
| **S** | Self-employed, directors | 4.1% | n/a (paid as part of self-assessment) |
| **K** | Pensions, investments only | 4% | n/a |
| **M** | No liability | 0% | n/a |

**PRSI minimum payment self-employed:** €650/year flat
**Class A weekly threshold:** earnings under €352/week → no PRSI

### Proprietary Directors
- Anyone with **>15% direct/indirect shareholding** = **proprietary director**
- Pay tax via **self-assessment Form 11**, NOT PAYE Modernisation
- Annual filing deadline: **31 October** (paper) / **mid-November** via ROS (Pay & File)
- **Preliminary tax:** 90% of current year OR 100% of prior year, due 31 Oct
- PAYE still deducted at source on salary, but final reconciliation via Form 11

### PAYE Modernisation
- Real-time reporting via ROS — every pay run reported same day
- **RPN (Revenue Payroll Notification):** retrieved per employee before each pay run
- **Payroll Submission:** sent on or before pay date
- **Monthly statement:** Revenue issues to employer; pays by 23rd of following month (direct debit) or 14th (other)
- Use compliant payroll software (Brightpay, Thesaurus, Sage, etc.)

### Pension contributions and tax relief
| Age | Max % of net relevant earnings |
|-----|-------------------------------|
| <30 | 15% |
| 30-39 | 20% |
| 40-49 | 25% |
| 50-54 | 30% |
| 55-59 | 35% |
| 60+ | 40% |

- **Net relevant earnings cap:** €115,000 (so max contribution at 40% = €46,000)
- Employer contributions: unlimited (within Revenue limits)
- Tax relief at marginal rate
- **Auto-enrolment** starting 2026 — confirm latest implementation date

## PART 5 — BENEFIT IN KIND (BIK)

### Company cars (2024+ rules — based on CO2 emissions)
| Category | CO2 (g/km) | BIK % @ annual mileage band 1 (≤26,000km) | Band 4 (>52,000km) |
|----------|------------|------------------------------------------|---------------------|
| **A** | 0-59 (EV/PHEV) | 22.5% | 11.25% |
| **B** | 60-79 | 26.25% | 13.125% |
| **C** | 80-99 | 30% | 15% |
| **D** | 100-119 | 33.75% | 16.875% |
| **E** | 120-139 | 37.5% | 18.75% |
| **F/G** | 140+ | 41.25% | 20.625% |

- **OMV** (Original Market Value) is the basis
- **EV temporary reduction:** €35,000 reduction in OMV for BIK calculation (2024-2025, reduced annually)
- **Van BIK:** 8% of OMV (flat rate, no mileage adjustment)
- **Pool cars:** no BIK if genuinely used by multiple employees, kept at premises overnight

### Small Benefit Exemption (vouchers)
- **€1,500/year** in non-cash benefits (vouchers, gifts) — up to 5 vouchers, one of which can be up to €1,500 cap
- Increased from €1,000 in Budget 2025
- Must be non-cash (vouchers, not bank transfers)
- Tax-free, no BIK

### Cycle to Work Scheme
- **€1,500** for e-bikes
- **€1,250** for regular bikes
- **€3,000** for cargo bikes (e-bikes & non-electric)
- Tax-free salary sacrifice over up to 12 months
- Bike must be used mainly for commuting

### Accommodation
- Living accommodation provided by employer → BIK on rental value
- Some exemptions for caretakers, "necessary" accommodation

### Loans
- Preferential rate loans: BIK = (Revenue specified rate − actual rate) × loan balance
- 2024 specified rate: 4% for non-qualifying, 13.5% for qualifying home loan

### Medical Insurance
- Employer pays premium → BIK on gross amount
- Employee gets 20% tax relief on net amount paid by employer

## PART 6 — CAPITAL TAXES

### Capital Gains Tax (CGT)
- **Standard rate:** 33%
- **Annual exemption:** €1,270/year
- **Filing:** Form CG1 (PAYE) or in Form 11 (self-assessed)
- **Payment dates:** 15 Dec for disposals 1 Jan – 30 Nov; 31 Jan for disposals 1-31 Dec

#### CGT reliefs
- **Principal Private Residence Relief:** main home exempt
- **Retirement Relief:** age 55+, qualifying business assets, full relief up to €750k (€500k from age 66+); marginal relief above; €10m cap on family transfers if under 66, no cap over 66
- **Entrepreneur Relief:** **10% rate** on first **€1m** lifetime gains from disposal of qualifying business
- **Transfer between spouses:** no CGT
- **Indexation:** abolished for disposals from 1 Jan 2003

### Capital Acquisitions Tax (CAT) — Gifts & Inheritance
- **Rate:** 33% above thresholds

**Group thresholds (lifetime):**
| Group | Relationship | Threshold (2025) |
|-------|-------------|------------------|
| A | Parent → child (incl. step, adopted) | **€400,000** |
| B | Sibling, niece/nephew, grandparent | **€40,000** |
| C | All others | **€20,000** |

**Reliefs:**
- **Small gift exemption:** €3,000/year from each disponer
- **Business Relief:** 90% on qualifying business assets (if held 2+ years)
- **Agricultural Relief:** 90% on qualifying farm assets
- **Dwelling House Exemption:** main residence relief for successor

### Stamp Duty
- **Shares:** 1% on Irish-listed/registered company shares
- **Residential property:** 1% up to €1m, 2% balance, **15%** on bulk purchases (10+ houses)
- **Non-residential property:** 7.5%
- **Leases > 35 years:** ad valorem stamp duty

### DIRT (Deposit Interest Retention Tax)
- **33%** on interest from Irish deposits
- Withheld at source by bank
- Final liability (not refundable normally) — exempt for over-65s under certain conditions

### LPT (Local Property Tax)
- Self-assessed, paid annually
- Bands based on property valuation
- Rates: 0.0906% on first €1.05m, 0.25% on excess

## PART 7 — RCT (Relevant Contracts Tax) — Construction sector

### What is RCT?
- Withholding tax for **principal contractors** paying **subcontractors** in construction, forestry, meat processing
- Applied via **eRCT** on ROS

### Rates
| Subcontractor compliance | RCT rate |
|--------------------------|----------|
| Good (full compliance, 3+ years filings) | **0%** |
| Standard | **20%** |
| Non-compliant | **35%** |

### Workflow
1. Principal must register contract on ROS before first payment
2. For each payment, lodge "Payment Notification" on ROS
3. Revenue returns a "Deduction Authorization" with applicable rate
4. Withhold accordingly, pay subcontractor net
5. File monthly RCT return (28th of following month) and pay withheld amount
6. Subcontractor sees withheld tax credited on their own ROS account

### RCT + VAT in construction
- **B2B construction services between principal + subcontractor:** VAT reverse charge applies (subcontractor invoices VAT-exclusive; principal accounts for 13.5% reverse charge)
- This is separate from RCT — both apply simultaneously

## PART 8 — WITHHOLDING TAXES

### Professional Services Withholding Tax (PSWT)
- **20%** withheld at source by **public bodies/government** paying for professional services (legal, accountancy, IT consultancy, etc.)
- The professional reclaims it via their CT/IT return
- Public body issues an F45 form

### Dividend Withholding Tax (DWT)
- **25%** withheld by Irish company on dividends
- Recipients with Irish PPS get credit
- Non-residents: subject to applicable Double Tax Treaty rate

### Construction RCT: see Part 7

## PART 9 — KEY RELIEFS & INCENTIVES

### R&D Tax Credit (see Part 3 — Corporation Tax)

### KEEP (Key Employee Engagement Programme)
- SMEs grant share options to key employees
- Gain on exercise: subject to CGT (33%) instead of income tax/USC/PRSI (52%)
- Caps and rules; suited to high-growth tech/SaaS
- Confirm with tax advisor — significant compliance

### EIIS (Employment & Investment Incentive Scheme)
- Investors get income tax relief (up to **40%**) on equity investments in qualifying SMEs
- Investor must hold for 4 years
- Annual cap €500k per investor
- Company must be SME, < €15m raised, qualifying trading activity

### SARP (Special Assignee Relief Programme)
- Reduces taxable income for executives assigned from abroad to Ireland
- 30% deduction on income > €100k (capped at €1m income)
- 5-year availability per individual

### FED (Foreign Earnings Deduction)
- For employees travelling to qualifying countries (BRICS, sub-Saharan Africa, others)
- Deduction up to €35k from taxable income
- Min 30 days abroad in 12 months

### Start-Up Refunds for Entrepreneurs (SURE)
- Income tax refund for individuals starting their own company
- Up to 6 prior years' PAYE refundable as investment

### Section 481 (Film Relief)
- 32% tax credit on qualifying film production expenditure
- Specific to film/TV/animation production

### Section 110 (Securitisation companies)
- Specialised regime for securitisation vehicles — outside SME scope normally

# Registration, composition and small-taxpayer schemes

LAST_VERIFIED: 2026-07-29
VOLATILE: thresholds, the simplified registration route, and state-specific
threshold notifications.

## Registration thresholds (§22)

| Supply type | Normal states | Special category states |
|---|---|---|
| Goods only | ₹40 lakh | ₹20 lakh |
| Services, or goods and services | ₹20 lakh | ₹10 lakh |

Special category states for this purpose include Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland and
Tripura at the lower figure, with several north-eastern states and Uttarakhand
having opted for the higher one. **The state-wise position is set by notification
and states have exercised different options** — verify for the state in question
rather than applying a general rule.

Threshold is computed on **aggregate turnover**: all taxable, exempt, export and
inter-state supplies of all persons under the **same PAN across India**, excluding
inward RCM supplies and GST itself. A business with ₹15 lakh in two states has
₹30 lakh of aggregate turnover and may be liable in both.

## Compulsory registration regardless of turnover (§24)

- Inter-state taxable supply of **goods** (services have a threshold exemption up to
  ₹20 lakh for inter-state supply).
- Casual taxable persons and non-resident taxable persons.
- Persons liable to pay under **reverse charge**.
- E-commerce operators required to collect TCS, and persons supplying through an
  e-commerce operator required to collect TCS (with an exemption for small
  suppliers of goods in specified circumstances).
- Persons liable to deduct TDS under §51.
- Input Service Distributors.
- Agents supplying on behalf of another taxable person.
- Persons supplying OIDAR services from outside India to unregistered recipients
  in India.

## Registration process

Form **REG-01** in two parts, PAN and Aadhaar based. Aadhaar authentication gives
faster processing; without it, physical verification of premises follows.
Non-risky applicants are generally granted registration within a short window;
risk-flagged applications take longer and attract site verification.

**Simplified/optional registration route (Rule 14A)** — from 1 November 2025, small
taxpayers whose monthly output tax liability is expected not to exceed ₹2.5 lakh
can opt for an automated route with approval in about three working days. It is
optional and has withdrawal conditions; check the current notification and the
consequences of exceeding the limit before recommending it.

Documents typically required: PAN, Aadhaar, proof of constitution, proof of
principal place of business, bank account details (which may be furnished after
registration within the prescribed period), photographs and authorisation.

**Amendment** in REG-14, **cancellation** in REG-16, **revocation of cancellation**
in REG-21 within the prescribed period. On cancellation, **GSTR-10** must be filed
within three months, and ITC on stock in hand must be reversed under §29(5).

## Composition scheme (§10)

| Category | Turnover limit | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Manufacturers and traders | ₹1.5 crore (₹75 lakh in specified special category states) | 1% (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST) |
| Restaurants (not serving alcohol) | ₹1.5 crore | 5% (2.5% + 2.5%) |
| Service providers and mixed suppliers under §10(2A) | ₹50 lakh | 6% (3% + 3%) |

For **traders**, the 1% applies to the turnover of **taxable** supplies only; for
manufacturers it applies to total turnover in the state. This distinction is
frequently missed.

**Conditions.** No inter-state **outward** supplies. No supplies through an
e-commerce operator required to collect TCS. Not a casual or non-resident taxable
person. No supply of goods notified as ineligible (ice cream, pan masala, tobacco,
aerated water, fly ash bricks and similar). A manufacturer or trader under §10(1)
may also supply services up to the higher of ₹5 lakh or 10% of turnover.

**Consequences.** No ITC on inputs. Cannot charge GST on the invoice — must issue a
**bill of supply** carrying the words "composition taxable person, not eligible to
collect tax on supplies". Must display that status on the signboard and on every
notice board at the place of business. **RCM applies at normal rates and is paid
in cash** — the composition rate does not cover inward reverse-charge supplies.

**Compliance.** CMP-08 quarterly by the 18th; GSTR-4 annually by 30 June following
the FY. Opt in via **CMP-02** by 31 March preceding the year, with **ITC-03** to
reverse credit on stock held. On crossing the limit, file **CMP-04** and move to
regular within the prescribed time, and **ITC-01** to claim credit on stock.

**Whether composition is actually better** is an arithmetic question, not a default.
It costs the customer's ITC, so B2B businesses usually lose more in lost custom
than they save in tax; it is generally attractive for B2C with low margins and
low input tax. Run the numbers for the specific business rather than recommending
it on turnover alone.

## QRMP

Optional for taxpayers with AATO up to ₹5 crore in the preceding FY. Mechanics are
in `02-returns-and-due-dates.md`. The trade-off worth explaining:

- Fewer returns (4 GSTR-1 + 4 GSTR-3B instead of 12 + 12), but tax is still paid
  monthly through PMT-06, so there is no cash-flow benefit.
- Without an IFF, B2B customers do not see the invoice in their GSTR-2B until the
  quarter ends, delaying their credit by up to two months. Customers notice. For a
  B2B business, filing the IFF monthly is usually worth the effort — it gives the
  return-count saving without the customer cost.
- Opting in or out is done per quarter, within the window preceding it, and stays
  effective until changed.

## Other small-taxpayer points

- A person supplying **goods** through an e-commerce operator must generally
  register regardless of turnover; the exemption for small suppliers is narrow and
  conditional. Check the current notification.
- **Casual taxable persons** (exhibitions, seasonal stalls) register for up to 90
  days, extendable, with advance deposit of estimated tax.
- **Voluntary registration** below the threshold is available and is often the right
  choice for a B2B supplier whose customers need the credit — but it brings the
  full compliance burden, including nil returns, and the three-year bar applies to
  those nil returns too.

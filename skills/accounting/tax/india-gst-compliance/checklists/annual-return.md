# Pre-filing checklist — GSTR-9 / GSTR-9C

GSTR-9 cannot be revised. Everything below happens before it is filed.

## Applicability and scope

- [ ] AATO for the FY computed across **all GSTINs under the PAN**, all India
- [ ] GSTR-9 applicability confirmed (mandatory above ₹2 crore)
- [ ] GSTR-9C applicability confirmed (mandatory above ₹5 crore, self-certified)
- [ ] Where GSTR-9 is optional, the decision to file or not is deliberate — note
      that filing it before 30 November **advances the §16(4) ITC cut-off**
- [ ] All 12 GSTR-1s and GSTR-3Bs for the year are filed
- [ ] Which tables are optional for **this specific FY** confirmed from the
      governing notification — this changes almost every year

## Data assembly

- [ ] System-computed GSTR-9 downloaded
- [ ] All GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and GSTR-2B data for the year downloaded to `source/`
- [ ] April–November returns of the **following** FY downloaded (for Part V)
- [ ] Audited financial statements for the year
- [ ] Electronic cash, credit and liability ledgers for the year
- [ ] Trial balance and expense ledgers (for the ITC and RCM sweeps)

## Turnover reconciliation

- [ ] Books turnover → GSTR-1 → GSTR-3B → GSTR-9 Tables 4 and 5, tied
- [ ] Every reconciling item listed and quantified: unbilled revenue, revenue of
      another year, Schedule I supplies, discounts, credit notes, income booked net
- [ ] Nil-rated, exempt and non-GST supplies separately reported
- [ ] Zero-rated supplies agreed to shipping bills / FIRCs
- [ ] Amendments made in the following year's April–November returns captured in
      Part V, and not double-counted in Part II

## ITC reconciliation

- [ ] Books ITC → GSTR-3B Table 4A → GSTR-9 Table 6, tied
- [ ] Reversals in Table 7 complete: Rules 37, 37A, 42, 43, §17(5)
- [ ] **Table 8** difference between 2A/2B and ITC availed analysed line by line,
      with a recorded reason for each component
- [ ] Credit of the year availed in the following April–November reported in Part V
- [ ] Nothing claimed that is time-barred under §16(4)

## Liability and payment

- [ ] Tax paid per GSTR-3B agreed to the ledgers, Table 9
- [ ] Additional liability identified and quantified
- [ ] Additional liability paid through **DRC-03 in cash**, with interest, **before**
      filing — it cannot be paid from the credit ledger through GSTR-9
- [ ] DRC-03A filed where the payment relates to a confirmed demand

## Year-end sweeps

- [ ] Rule 42/43 annual finalisation, with interest on any shortfall
- [ ] RCM completeness for the whole year re-tested — this is the last practical
      chance before an audit
- [ ] Credit notes relating to the year but issued later, within the §34(2) limit
- [ ] Job work: goods sent and not returned within 1 year (inputs) or 3 years
      (capital goods) — deemed supply, Table 16
- [ ] Stock and ITC-04 position reconciled

## HSN summaries

- [ ] Table 17 (outward) complete at the correct digit count
- [ ] Table 18 (inward) — check whether it is optional for this FY before building
      it
- [ ] HSN values reconcile to the turnover and ITC figures

## GSTR-9C

- [ ] Financial statements are entity-level; GSTR-9C is GSTIN-level — the
      apportionment working is documented
- [ ] Part A turnover reconciliation complete, unreconciled differences explained
      rather than left as a residual
- [ ] Part B tax reconciliation complete
- [ ] Part C ITC reconciliation complete; Table 14 (expense-head-wise) built only if
      required for this FY
- [ ] Additional liability in Part D consistent with the DRC-03 already paid
- [ ] Self-certification understood by the person signing it

## External consistency

- [ ] Turnover per GSTR-9 vs audited financials, reconciled
- [ ] Turnover vs income tax return and Form 26AS / AIS, differences explained
- [ ] Any difference the department could cross-check is documented **before** they
      ask

## Sign-off

- [ ] Difference register closed or carried with reasons
- [ ] Filing pack prepared, with the source of every figure
- [ ] Named reviewer has signed off
- [ ] User reminded that GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C cannot be revised
